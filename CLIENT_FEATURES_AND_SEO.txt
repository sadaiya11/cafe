BUN MASKA CAFE - PROJECT FEATURES, SEO, AND FUTURE ROADMAP
============================================================

CLIENT PRESENTATION SUMMARY

1. CUSTOMER WEBSITE FEATURES
-----------------------------
- Café homepage with hero banners and promotional sections.
- Online food menu with categories:
  - Bun Maska
  - Sandwiches
  - Maggie
  - Momos
  - French Fries
  - Beverages
- Product detail pages with product name, description, price, images,
  availability, quantity selector, add-to-cart, and buy-now actions.
- Product variants and variant pricing support.
- Customer reviews and ratings display.
- Shopping cart and checkout.
- Customer registration, login, logout, forgot password, and reset password.
- Customer profile and order history.
- Order status notifications.
- Responsive mobile and desktop design.
- About Us, Contact Us, and Special Offers pages.

2. ONLINE ORDERING AND PAYMENT
------------------------------
- Razorpay payment integration structure.
- Test payment support.
- Demo payment mode when the payment API is unavailable.
- Payment success and payment failure screens.
- Backend order creation and payment verification.
- Customer order history and status tracking.
- Server-side validation for order and payment data.

Live Razorpay credentials must be configured and tested before launch.

3. ADMIN PANEL FEATURES
-----------------------
The admin panel allows staff to manage:
- Orders and order status.
- Products, prices, descriptions, categories, tags, and availability.
- Product images and gallery images.
- Inventory.
- Coupons and discounts.
- Customers.
- Staff accounts and permissions.
- Customer reviews.
- Payments.
- Store settings.
- Hero banners and promotional slides.
- Analytics and best-selling products.
- CSV sales exports.
- Order notification sounds and alerts.

Admin and staff access is protected with authentication and role-based access.

4. POINT-OF-SALE SYSTEM
------------------------
The project includes a POS system for café staff:
- POS menu and product search.
- Add items to an active ticket.
- Quantity management.
- POS checkout.
- Payment method selection.
- Thermal receipt display.
- Shift summary.
- Daily register information.

5. BACKEND AND DATABASE
-----------------------
Technology used:
- React and Vite for the frontend.
- Node.js and Express for backend APIs.
- PostgreSQL database through Supabase.
- Prisma for database management.
- JWT authentication.
- bcrypt password hashing.
- Razorpay payments.
- Supabase Storage for product and banner images.
- Resend/Nodemailer for email functionality.

The database stores:
- Customers.
- Admin and staff users.
- Products and product variants.
- Orders and order items.
- Store settings.
- Hero banners.

6. SEO FEATURES IMPLEMENTED
----------------------------
Page titles:
- Each important page can have its own SEO title.
- Product pages use the product name in the page title.

Meta descriptions:
- Pages have descriptions for search engines.
- Descriptions mention Bun Maska, Maggie, noodles, momos, French fries,
  snacks, café food, and Kinley water.

SEO keywords:
- Bun Maska Café.
- Bun Maska online.
- Vegetarian café.
- Maggi and noodles.
- Momos.
- French fries.
- Café food delivery.
- Kinley water.
- Café in Guna.

Keywords support the page but useful content, local information, links,
reviews, and website authority are more important to Google.

Canonical URLs:
- Canonical URLs identify the official version of each page.
- This helps prevent duplicate URL issues.

Robots metadata:
- Public pages are configured for indexing.
- Private pages such as cart, checkout, login, profile, and orders should not
  be indexed by search engines.

Open Graph metadata:
- Social sharing title.
- Social sharing description.
- Social sharing image.
- Social sharing URL.

Twitter/X metadata:
- Twitter/X card title, description, and image.

Structured data / JSON-LD:
- Café business information.
- Café name, phone, address, opening hours, and cuisine types.
- Product names, categories, prices, images, and availability.
- Social profile links through the schema.org sameAs property.

Sitemap:
- Homepage.
- Menu page.
- About page.
- Contact page.
- Offers page.
- Individual product pages.

Robots.txt:
- Allows public pages to be crawled.
- Blocks private customer pages.
- Points search engines to the sitemap.

Social SEO:
- Instagram, Facebook, X/Twitter, YouTube, WhatsApp, and Google Business
  links can be configured in Admin > Store Settings.
- These links appear in the website footer and structured data.
- Replace all example social URLs with the café's official URLs.

7. DEPLOYMENT ARCHITECTURE

10. SHORT CLIENT EXPLANATION
----------------------------
This project is an online café ordering platform with a customer website,
shopping cart, checkout, payments, customer accounts, order tracking, admin
management, POS billing, product management, analytics, and SEO support.

The SEO setup includes page titles, descriptions, keywords, canonical URLs,
Open Graph sharing data, Twitter/X cards, product structured data, café
structured data, social profile connections, sitemap.xml, and robots.txt.

The next important steps are adding the final business information, connecting
real social profiles, verifying Google Business Profile, configuring live
payments, submitting the sitemap to Google Search Console, and securing all
production credentials.
